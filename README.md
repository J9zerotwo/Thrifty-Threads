# Thrifty-Threads
Thrifty Threads, as a dedicated online platform for second-hand or pre-loved clothing and items, offers a range of features to enhance the shopping experience for its customers.  These features combined create a convenient and secure online shopping experience specifically tailored to the trading of pre-loved clothing, accessories, shoes, and more.

## User Accounts
* Thrifty Threads provides the option for customers to create user accounts. By creating an account, customers can save their personal information, shipping addresses, and payment details, which simplifies the checkout process for future purchases. User accounts may also grant access to order history and tracking information.

## Shopping Cart
* Customers can add selected items, track quantities and prices, and review their selections before checkout. It includes a checkbox feature for customers to choose specific products for purchase and a remove button to easily eliminate unwanted items from the cart. These enhancements provide customers with greater control and flexibility in managing their shopping experience, ensuring they can make modifications as needed and proceed to checkout with confidence.
